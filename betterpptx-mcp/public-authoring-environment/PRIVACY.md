# Privacy boundary

This repository contains public authoring guidance only. It does not receive or
store presentation content.

Data handling for a connected BetterPPTX service is governed by the service and
distribution policies presented to the user. Do not infer retention, training,
or deletion promises from this curriculum alone.

Do not place presentation data, access keys, customer information, private
assets, or service credentials in public issues or curriculum contributions.
Use the private support and privacy channels identified by the product's
distribution page.
